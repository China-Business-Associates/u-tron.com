The U-Tron Heritage: From Central Beijing to Global Thoroughfares

U-Tron (Beijing) Electronics Co. Ltd. was established in 1992 as a Wholly Owned Foreign Enterprise (WOFE) in Beijing, China, fully funded by America-China Business Associates (CBA) to localize high-technology electronics, computing, and communications equipment.

Originally established on the 5th floor of the historic #2 Printing Factory of Beijing, U-Tron entered into a 25-year lease for a 1,000-square-meter precision production floor. As the district transformed into Beijing's prime central business district, municipal urban planning prompted the relocation of manufacturing operations to the Beijing Economic-Technological Development Area (BDA) in Yizhuang, where U-Tron operates a dedicated 2,000 m² production and research campus.

Proprietary Engineering

Technological Innovations from U-Tron R&D

A showcase of patented thermodynamic designs, smart municipal control hardware, and extreme climate durability.

Thermodynamics • Patented

Deon-Cooler 6063-T5 Extrusion

Proprietary thermodynamic heat-dissipation geometry fabricated from 6063-T5 aircraft-grade aluminum alloy. Eliminates motorized cooling fans and guarantees junction temperatures under 65°C for 50,000-hour L70 life.

Operating Envelope: -40°C to +45°C • Verified in Gothenburg Swedish winter and tropical desert climates.
Smart City • Power Line Carrier

6800-LCM-R1 Central Light Control

Centralized municipal lighting management unit controlling 16 to 512 channels over power-line carrier (PLC) and 0-10V/PWM. Mounts inside existing power distribution cabinets with remote CDMA and Internet access.

Telemetry Features: 365-day event recording for brownouts and blackouts • Automated light intensity reduction scheduling.
ITS • IoT Convergence

CarLED with CamEye Surveillance

Convergence of high-mast area illumination with municipal security. Integrates an HDTV Power-over-Ethernet (POE) IP surveillance camera and wireless mesh antenna directly onto high-power 300W flood fixtures.

Architecture: IP67 POE camera feed + outdoor Wi-Fi SSID broadcast + NEMA dusk-to-dawn twist-lock photocell.
Substrate Engineering

Al-Cu-Ceramic Substrate (14mm Moon Light)

Multi-layer Aluminum-Copper-Ceramic metal-core PCB technology enabling ultra-low thermal resistance. Formulated the world's thinnest commercial architectural downlights at just 14 mm total fixture depth.

Performance: 2,000 lm from 100 Cree chips at just 12 Watts • Zero hotspot degradation.
Optical Modularity

LeDiwali Modular Engine Architecture

Lightweight modular aluminum luminaire engines (14A, 14B, 12 Bar) designed for rapid assembly. Features asymmetric batwing roadway optical lenses and interchangeable 700mA constant-current driver configurations.

Modularity: 20W to 30W building blocks • Up to 3 modules combined to match high-pressure sodium (HPS) illumination.
Off-Grid Power

DRiBox Turnkey Microgrid Packages

Modular off-grid solar kits (Packages 1, 2, and 3) housing weatherproof battery enclosures, MPPT charge controllers, monocrystalline solar panels, 12V DC Helios LED bulbs, and USB device charging hubs.

Autonomy: Guaranteed 10 hours of operation for 3 consecutive overcast nights without direct solar charging.
